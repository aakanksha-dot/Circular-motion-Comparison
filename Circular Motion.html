Horizontal vs Vertical Circles

Two fundamental modes of circular motion: a horizontal conical pendulum operating at zero tangential acceleration, alongside a vertical circular loop governed by gravity and conservation of energy.

1. Horizontal Circular Motion (Conical Pendulum)

  • Fixed Angle & Radius (ΣFy = 0). Vertical forces perfectly balance (T cosθ = mg), maintaining a constant plane of motion.
  • Pure Horizontal Centripetal Force. T sinθ provides ac straight to the center. Speed is constant, so tangential acceleration at = 0.

2. Vertical Circular Motion

In a vertical loop, gravity acts downward throughout the path. Gravity accelerates the object along the descending arc (green) and decelerates it along the ascending arc (rust), changing both speed and tension dynamically.
